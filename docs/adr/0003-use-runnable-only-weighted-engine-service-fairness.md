# Use Runnable-Only Weighted Engine Service Fairness

Model Weight and the Model Ledger attach to a stable Model ID, so all of its Model Revisions, Work Kinds, and requests share one cross-model fair-service account. Each ledger advances by actual Engine Service divided by Model Weight, and normal fair selection chooses the Runnable Model with the smallest aligned ledger. Under the recorded P-1A YELLOW result, Engine Service is synchronized Device Executor service and must not be reported as MLX command-buffer service time or GPU occupancy. Idle or Suspended Models do not bank service; a returning Model, safety resume, and Weight Change align to the current baseline, changing only future service rate. Individual Standard and Background requests accrue Progress Bound time only while safe and runnable; Warming, Suspension, and Output Backpressure pause visibly, and resume establishes a new remaining timing obligation without stored priority credit.
