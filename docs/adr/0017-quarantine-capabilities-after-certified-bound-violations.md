# Quarantine Capabilities After Certified Bound Violations

An actual execution-time or resource-use value exceeding its Certification Record upper bound is a contract violation rather than a normal estimate miss. The completed operation still produces its authoritative Receipt, but the most specific affected Capability Key - Model Revision, phase, batch and shape bucket, Adapter and MLX builds, Backend Interface revision, and Envelope - is removed from shared Candidate Formation and emits an auditable violation. Correlated failures may explicitly escalate quarantine to a wider parent scope; one failure does not disable unrelated keys. Re-entry requires explicit recertification rather than automatic bound expansion. Exceeding only an ordinary Cost Profile estimate updates that estimate within the unchanged certified bound and does not quarantine. Any certified upper-bound update invalidates old candidates and descriptions and triggers Profile Revalidation of existing Reservations; newly infeasible timing obligations emit SLO Risk and stop related new Admission. A resource-bound violation also triggers immediate Governor reevaluation.
