# Use a Compositional Bounded Integrity Lifecycle

This decision defines the later Integrity Serving Profile and is not a prerequisite for the P0 Serving Profile. P0 retains authoritative durable Control Mutation, bounded non-recovery Audit, fail-closed schema handling, and restart with empty inference state, but advertises no Identity Anchor, Bundle, Restore, Clone, multi-authority repair, Compatibility Custody, or Bounded Integrity Lifecycle capability.

A derived failure receives no new glossary term or Lifecycle specialization when it introduces no new authority meaning, external Effect rule, physical ownership or capacity rule, or Schema identity. It is represented by the existing compositional Lifecycle, qualification fixtures and bound decision or Transition Table; a new term is reserved for a genuinely new domain boundary rather than a new combination of existing failures.

Reader retirement now requires two failure-domain-independent Jobs to prove complete dependency drain and exact Compatibility Tool readback through both custody routes. Later loss of that Tool or either route enters the existing Evidence Custody Lifecycle only for the exact Tool, dependent historical queries and future retirement proofs; current authority already proved dependency-free remains outside the incident. Later exact qualified reader, Schema, Catalog and Composition Proof support for unchanged bytes permits only a fresh Applicability Proof to clear the current Migration No Path while preserving its historical fact. Forward Reconciliation accepts only the original transition's native publication identity, Receipt and authoritative read-only readback interpreted identically by two independent Jobs.

Late Applicability omission derives every direct and transitive affected Effect, cancels proven-unexposed Effects and restricts started or exposure-ambiguous Effects to exact-operation read-only reconciliation into nonauthority. Alias Manifest recovery creates and fully qualifies a new generation while retaining the failed generation. Catalog successor publication serializes exactly one candidate against the exact current head, permanently makes every older inactive Catalog ineligible, leaves competing candidates nonauthoritative until rederived and requalified against the new head, and never automatically activates the successor.

Schema Vector authority requires cross-axis Composition Proof in addition to every per-axis proof, and reader retirement is blocked by every open or current authority binding. Required reader loss yields Migration No Path; Forward Reconciliation exhaustion yields Self-Integrity Terminal; late Applicability omission invalidates its Proof and dependent readiness. Alias Manifest failure stays inside migration-dependent qualification, while a published but Policy-inactive Catalog remains permanent and may be selected later through fresh independent activation only until a successor publishes.

An open Lifecycle is generation-pinned and never migrates; per-axis compatibility requires qualified full-fixture and reader-matrix proof. Transition publication uncertainty is nonready and resolves only forward to the original transition or Self-Integrity Terminal. Applicability reuse is exact-identity-bound, Formula invalidity either rejects before open or creates a complete dependent Integrity Incident, Alias Manifests never reinterpret history, and specialization publication failures preserve every already published step as forward-only authority.

Every Lifecycle object binds an independently versioned four-axis Schema Vector; each Catalog generation fixes one total Transition Table and qualified checked Capacity Formula per specialization. A new specialization crosses separate Schema, independent qualification, Catalog successor and Policy activation boundaries, while Applicability requires two failure-domain-independent derivations and Scenario Alias migration requires a complete independently verified append-only Manifest. Unknown or unsupported applicable specializations remain preserved evidence but produce Migration No Path and nonreadiness without fallback.

TurnVector represents every integrity recovery through one immutable Bounded Integrity Lifecycle identity, a closed qualified Specialization Catalog and Outcome Algebra, a complete pre-Effect Capacity Reserve, and a predeclared Self-Integrity Terminal that ends failure of the lifecycle itself without recursive lifecycle creation. Current-Tree readiness requires a complete fixed-universe Applicability Proof rather than a scan of known incidents or Policy whitelist. Existing long-form scenario terms remain exact historical Scenario Aliases while new architecture uses the canonical lifecycle vocabulary; bulk rewriting waits for a separately verified mapping.
